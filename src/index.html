About PathwayScope

PathwayScope is an interactive explorer for the Reactome pathway knowledgebase. Educational and research-exploration use only. It is not medical advice.

  • Pathway enrichment is hypothesis-generating, not proof.
  • Results depend on the background (the set of entities Reactome compares against) and on how identifiers map.
  • Human pathways only.

Data sources

  • Reactome Content and Analysis Services (data released under CC0).
  • UniProt for protein names and accessions.
  • NCBI ClinVar for pathogenic-record counts.
