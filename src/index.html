About PathwayScope

PathwayScope is an interactive explorer for the Reactome pathway knowledgebase. Educational and research-exploration use only. It is not medical advice.

What over-representation analysis does and does not tell you

  • It asks whether your genes land in a pathway more often than chance would predict, given a background set. It is hypothesis-generating, not proof.
  • The background matters: a different background gives different results. Very small and very large pathways behave differently (small ones are easy to hit by chance; large ones are broad).
  • FDR (false discovery rate) is the expected share of false hits among pathways called significant after testing many pathways at once.
  • Identifier mapping is imperfect. Unmapped identifiers are always listed, and the results describe only the mapped part of your list.
  • Human pathways only. Counts are of Reactome entities (proteins), so paralogs that share a protein can make "found" smaller than your gene count.

Data sources

  • Reactome Content and Analysis Services (data released under CC0).
  • UniProt for protein names and accessions.
  • NCBI ClinVar for pathogenic-record counts.
  • Links out only (nothing is fetched from them): AlphaFold DB and gnomAD.
